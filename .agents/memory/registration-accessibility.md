---
name: Registration accessibility
description: User concerns about anti-bot friction for older store customers.
---

Prefer unobtrusive registration protection and avoid adding visible CAPTCHA as a default.

**Why:** The user said many people ordering on the site are older and CAPTCHA would be an additional barrier. They want registration to remain available while curtailing automated account creation.

**How to apply:** Start with server-side limits that do not add form steps. Explain that IP limits cannot stop distributed bots; discuss the accessibility and potential interaction requirements of any additional challenge before introducing one.
